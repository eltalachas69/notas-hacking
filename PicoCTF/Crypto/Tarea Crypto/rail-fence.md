## Descripción
A type of transposition cipher is the rail fence cipher, which is described [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_fence_cipher). Here is one such cipher encrypted using the rail fence with 4 rails. Can you decrypt it? Download the message [here](https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/e3a0d625f6dd6554233d2675553060046f8bf5b21af03458091011ad1fccc3d7/message.txt).

The decoded message names a phrase. Put that phrase, and nothing else, in the academy flag format: `academy{phrase}`.
## Solución
Para resolver esto tuve que usar cyberchef y ya de ahi mismo le puse key 4 y me dio el contenido dentro de la bandera que es `academy{WH3R3_D035_7H3_F3NC3_8361N_4ND_3ND_D3AB175F}`
